Commission Tier Structure

The discount a job sold at maps the technician and Service Manager to a commission rate. Discount = how far below book price the job sold. Each tier ceiling carries a 0.1% rounding buffer — e.g. Tier 1 holds through 5.1%, and Tier 2 begins only above 5.1%. Same 0.1% tolerance applies at every tier break. The tier % is set by counting discounts only — benign credits do not move the tier. Commission is paid on the gross after ALL discounts are removed — counting and benign both — because we collect on what we actually collected. Benign credits don't hurt the tier, but we don't pay on them either. Non-commissionable revenue tasks (e.g. Smart Sadie) count as revenue but earn zero commission and are also excluded from the pay base — unless a matching credit line on the same invoice already zeroed the task out (net-net $0). In that case the discount already removed it from the sold price, so it is not carved out again.

How it works: The deeper the discount, the lower the commission rate for both the tech and the SM. Commission steps down as the discount deepens. Combined % shown is the blended Tech + SM payout for that tier.
SM sub-$3,000 rule: When a job's book price is under $3,000, the SM is paid a flat 2% of pay base — the discount tier is irrelevant to the SM on these jobs (even a zero-discount Tier 1 job pays the SM 2%, not 3%). Jobs at $3,000 book and above use the standard tier SM rate. The tech side is never affected by this rule.

Discount Treatment Matrix

Every discount is flagged benign (does not dock commission) or counts (treated as a real discount and run through the tier matrix). The Rule column states the condition a benign credit must meet to stay benign — if the rule isn't met, it counts. Benign items have no per-item % cap; they only flip to counting when the rule fails or the combined discount pushes a tier over its max. The finance fee (5%) is the only item carrying its own percentage cap — handled in its own section below.

Finance Fee

Finance Fee is treated like a discount.

5% max (5.1% buffered)

A finance fee behaves exactly like a discount once it breaks the 5% ceiling (the Tier 1 max). With the 0.1% rounding buffer, it stays benign through 5.1% and does not dock commission. The moment it exceeds 5.1%, the overage is treated as a real discount and folds into the combined discount that runs through the tier matrix — docking commission the same way any counting discount does.

The max rule, stated once: benign stays benign only while it's under its cap and the combined discount stays inside the tier max (each tier carries a 0.1% rounding buffer). Break either ceiling — including a finance fee over 5.1% — and it counts.

Non-Commissionable Revenue Tasks

Some revenue tasks count toward income but do not pay commission. They are not discounts — the money is collected and shows as revenue, but it is carved out of the commission pay base. These are matched by name (case-insensitive).

Revenue Task Treatment Rule
Any task containing "Smart Sadie" NO COMMISSION Counts as revenue. Excluded from the commission pay base — tech and SM are paid $0 on it. Exception: if a matching credit at the same price zeroed the task out on the invoice, it is NOT carved out again (the credit already removed it).
Smart Sadie, two ways: the Smart Sadie task (revenue) earns no commission and sits in this list. The Smart Sadie complimentary membership discount is benign only when a matching Smart Sadie task at the same price is on the job (the comp nets to zero). Without a matching task, that discount counts. And the pair nets clean: when task + matching credit are both on the invoice, the credit removes the $ from the sold price and the task is not carved out again — commission is paid on everything else. Example: job 547023, sold price $7,570 with a $155 Sadie task offset by its −$155 comp discount → pay base $7,570, not $7,415.

Payroll Checker

Paste the Master Pay File below. The tool parses every COM pay-code line, splits it into tech commission (activities with "Commissions" in the name) and SM commission (activities with "Sales SM"), folds in any matching Direct Adjustment COM line for the same person, and compares the result job-by-job against what the Commission tab says we should pay. Variance is cumulative: prior payments on the same job/person/role (from the Payroll History ledger) are folded in, so a job paid across several pay periods only flags if the running total exceeds the full should-pay. Matches and underpays are hidden; only overpays are listed. Each checked week is auto-appended to the ledger.

Payroll History (Cumulative Ledger)

A rolling 180-day ledger of every COM commission line ever paid, keyed by job # + person + role and dated per line. The Payroll Checker reads this ledger so a job paid across several pay periods nets correctly instead of flagging a false variance — true variance is full should-pay − (everything already paid on this job/person/role + this week). Older-than-180-day rows prune automatically on load. The ledger lives in this browser; use Export to back it up or move it to another machine.
